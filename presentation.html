05 · Security & Compliance

Healthcare Privacy & Role-Based Access Control

Guaranteeing confidentiality through proactive routing intercepts and sandboxed query filters.

🔒

Route Protection Guard

Guarded routes (dashboard.html, patients.html, treatments.html, followups.html) evaluate currentUser.role === 'doctor'. Unauthorized attempts redirect to login.html with an inline warning toast.

Zero Leakage
🗂️

Sandboxed Medical History

When logged in as a patient, history.html forces queries to filter strictly by currentPatientId. The patient cannot inspect, edit, or see any other patient's treatment plans or allergies.

HIPAA Alignment
🧼

XSS Defensive Sanitization

All dynamic user inputs are passed through an HTML entity encoder (esc()) before DOM injection, blocking Cross-Site Scripting vulnerabilities completely.

Secure DOM Injection

08 · Artificial Intelligence

ToothMate — AI Clinical Copilot

Dual-mode artificial intelligence engineered for chairside dental operations and patient reassurance.

🩺 Clinician Mode (SOAP & Pharmacology)

Doctor Mode

• Automated SOAP Notes: Formats Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan notes with working lengths and irrigation protocols.
• 1-Click EHR Injection: Extracted notes insert directly into active treatment records with a single click.
• Anesthetic Calculations: Maximum safe dosage calculations (e.g., Lignocaine 2% with 1:100k Epinephrine).
• Allergy Alternatives: Safe antibiotics for penicillin-allergic patients (Clindamycin, Azithromycin).

👤 Patient Mode (24/7 Triage & Guidance)

Patient Mode

• Post-Op Care Guidance: Immediate answers for dry socket prevention, soft food diets, and bleeding control.
• Demystifying Jargon: Plain-language explanations of why crowns are required after root canals.
• Emergency First-Aid: Step-by-step guidance for avulsed / knocked-out teeth (Hank's solution or cold milk).
• Hybrid Engine: Live Google Gemini API with fallback to offline clinical expert core.
